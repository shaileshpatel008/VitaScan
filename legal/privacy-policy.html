This policy explains how the Wellumi mobile app (“Wellumi”, “the app”), published by {{DEVELOPER_NAME}} (“we”, “us”), handles your information.

In short: Wellumi keeps your health information on your phone, encrypted. We have no account system and no server that receives your readings. We don’t sell, share or advertise with your data, and camera images are never saved.

1. Information you enter or create

  • Profile: name, age, gender, height, weight and optional health background (for example, whether you have high blood pressure). These improve the accuracy of estimates.
  • Readings: heart rate, heart-rate variability, stress score, breathing rate and blood pressure estimates from scans; blood pressure and pulse values you enter from a cuff; and any notes.
  • Settings: reminder times and app preferences.

2. Camera

When you start a scan, the app uses the rear camera and flash to detect the small changes in light caused by your pulse. Each camera frame is reduced on your phone to an average colour value and then discarded. No photos or video are recorded, stored or sent anywhere. The camera is used only while a scan is running.

3. Where your information is stored

All of the information above is stored only on your device, in an encrypted database. The encryption key is kept in your phone’s secure storage (Android Keystore / iOS Keychain). On Android, the app opts out of cloud backup, so this data is not copied to your Google backup. We do not receive or have access to it.

4. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your information with anyone. Information leaves your phone only when you choose to share it, for example by exporting a PDF report or CSV file or sharing a reading, using your phone’s share sheet. You decide who receives it.

5. Network connections

Wellumi works offline. It connects to the internet only to:

  • check for app updates through Google Play (on Android) or the App Store (on iOS). This tells the store which app and version is installed, but no personal or health information.
  • open links you tap, such as this policy, in your browser.

Wellumi contains no advertising, analytics, tracking or crash-reporting SDKs, and its fonts are built into the app.

7. Health platforms

This version of Wellumi does not connect to Google Health Connect or Apple Health. If we add this in the future, it will be optional, we will explain exactly which data is written, and we will update this policy before the feature is released. Data from those platforms will never be used for advertising.

8. Keeping and deleting your information

Your information stays on your phone until you delete it. You can delete individual readings, or delete everything at any time in Profile → Delete all my data. Uninstalling the app also removes all of its data from your phone. You can export your data first from Profile → Export all data (CSV).

9. Children

Wellumi is intended for adults aged 18 and over. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

10. Security

Readings are stored encrypted on your device. Please protect your phone with a screen lock, because anyone who can unlock your phone can open the app.

11. Your rights

Because your information stays on your device and we do not hold a copy, you have direct control over it: you can view, export, correct and delete it in the app at any time. If you have questions about privacy laws that apply to you (for example GDPR or India’s DPDP Act), contact us using the details below.

12. Not a medical device

Wellumi is a wellness app. It does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any disease, and its readings are estimates. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional about your health.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when we add features. We will post the new version on this page with a new effective date, and tell you in the app about important changes.
